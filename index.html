Assistant Professor

Department of Sociology and Social Policy
Lingnan University, Hong Kong

I study how social conditions shape health and well-being. My research focuses on mental health, substance abuse, health communication, and health inequality. I also examine crime and deviance, drawing on quantitative and mixed-methods approaches.
